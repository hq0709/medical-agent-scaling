\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our tasks are multiple-choice examination items, not clinical encounters. They carry no history
taking, no test ordering, no longitudinal follow-up, and the exact-match endpoint rewards the
option-selection component of diagnosis only. Results here do not extrapolate to tool-using
clinical agents.

The diversity analysis is limited to evaluated models from six vendors (OpenAI, Google,
Anthropic, DeepSeek, Alibaba and Zhipu; \S\ref{sec:premise}).

The specialty router ranks by relevance, so larger panels append progressively less relevant
specialists. Panel size is therefore entangled with specialty relevance. The \textsc{Generic
panel} control removes specialty roles by giving every member the same generic internist prompt,
separating panel size from specialty relevance (\S\ref{sec:premise}).
