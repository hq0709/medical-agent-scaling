\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Why pooled opinions help, and when they stop.}
Collective-intelligence results in medicine rest on error independence: pooling independent
diagnostic judgements improves accuracy~\citep{kurvers2016boosting, wolf2015collective},
plateauing at roughly five raters~\citep{kammer2017}, and groups of nine outperform individuals on
the Human Dx platform~\citep{barnett2019}. This is the clinical instance of a general
principle~\citep{surowiecki2004wisdom} that multidisciplinary tumour boards
institutionalise~\citep{radcliffe2019tumorboard}. The framing question is not whether two heads beat one
but when~\citep{hautz2024}. The same literature documents what happens when independence fails:
conformity to a unanimous majority~\citep{asch1956conformity} and groupthink in deliberating
groups~\citep{janis1972groupthink} are the human analogues of the correlation collapse we measure.
Correlated errors across models bound ensemble benefit in general~\citep{correlated2025}, and
analyses of multi-agent debate show consensus can converge on confident-but-wrong
answers~\citep{demystifying_mad}. Diagnostic error is common enough in ordinary
practice~\citep{graber2013diagnosticerror, singh2014frequency} that a consultation mechanism which
manufactures unwarranted agreement is a safety concern rather than an efficiency one.


\paragraph{Medical language models and their evaluation.}
Medical question answering became a benchmark for general capability once models began matching
licensing-exam thresholds~\citep{kung2023usmle, nori2023capabilities}, and Med-PaLM established
expert-level performance as an explicit target~\citep{singhal2023medpalm, singhal2025medpalm2}.
Subsequent work moved from answering questions to conducting encounters, with AMIE demonstrating
diagnostic dialogue and differential-diagnosis generation at physician
level~\citep{tu2025amie, mcduff2025amie}. Prompting strategy alone carries much of this
progress~\citep{nori2024medprompt}, which is precisely why architectural claims require
budget-matched controls. A panel must be shown to beat the same model prompted harder, not merely
to beat the same model prompted once.

\paragraph{Medical multi-agent systems.}
MedAgents~\citep{tang2024medagents} introduced role-played specialist collaboration;
MDAgents~\citep{kim2024mdagents} added adaptive routing between solo and group modes;
MAC~\citep{chen2025mac} and TeamMedAgents~\citep{teammedagents} formalised teamwork components,
the latter sweeping team sizes 2--5 under a Pareto-efficiency framing. Every published medical
ablation stops at $N \leq 5$, none reports a dose--response curve, and none runs a budget-matched
single-model control.

\paragraph{Skeptical evaluations.}
MedAgentBoard~\citep{medagentboard} and a large npj Digital Medicine benchmarking
study~\citep{npjdm2026} independently report that multi-agent orchestration frequently fails to
beat well-prompted single models on clinical tasks at substantially higher cost.
ConfAgents~\citep{confagents} quantifies the overhead at roughly $50\times$ the latency of a single
generation for marginal gain. Our economics analysis extends this line quantitatively, and our
independence measurement supplies the mechanism it lacks.

\paragraph{Multi-agent coordination in general domains.}
Debate-style protocols improve factuality on reasoning tasks~\citep{du2024debate} and are argued
to counteract degeneration of thought through enforced divergence~\citep{liang2024encouraging};
agent frameworks such as MetaGPT~\citep{hong2024metagpt} and AutoGen~\citep{wu2024autogen} have
made these patterns easy to deploy, and systematic studies compare collaboration
mechanisms~\citep{zhang2024exploring}. \citet{kim2026scaling} is the most complete treatment: a
predictive scaling principle fitted across five architectures, three model families and six
agentic benchmarks under matched budgets, from which the 45\% capability ceiling and the
error-amplification asymmetry are derived. We replicate its design in the medical, tool-free
regime.

\paragraph{Scaling and test-time compute.}
Parameter and data scaling laws~\citep{kaplan2020scaling, hoffmann2022chinchilla} have analogues
at inference time, where repeated sampling~\citep{brown2024monkeys} and adaptive test-time
compute~\citep{snell2024scaling} trade computation for accuracy. ``More agents is all you
need''~\citep{li2024more} reported monotone gains from ensemble size;
\citet{chen2024morecalls} showed the relationship is non-monotone, with an inverted-U in the
number of LLM calls on hard instances. \citet{mas_scaling2606} confirmed the inverted-U in agent
count, and \citet{diversity2602} attributed the fast-then-slow shape to opinion diversity, showing
two diverse agents can match sixteen homogeneous ones. Self-consistency~\citep{wang2023selfconsistency}
over chain-of-thought~\citep{wei2022cot} is the natural budget-matched control for any such claim,
and is the control we run at every cell.

\paragraph{Confidence and calibration.}
Our safety analysis depends on whether a stated confidence is informative. Language models are
partially aware of what they know~\citep{kadavath2022know}, but elicited confidence is weakly
calibrated and sensitive to how it is asked~\citep{xiong2024can}, a failure mode with a long
history in neural classifiers~\citep{guo2024calibration}. Clinical evaluations report fluent but
unreliable responses~\citep{goodman2024accuracy}, and demographic biases propagate through
medical LLM outputs~\citep{omiye2023bias, zack2024bias}. We add a coordination-specific finding:
deliberation degrades the discriminative power of confidence rather than improving it.
