\subsection{Agents and multi-agent systems}
A MAS comprises 9 instances of one VLM (\cref{fig:design}).
The single agent uses a general-radiologist prompt; self-consistency samples that prompt with majority vote~\cite{wang2023selfconsistency}.
Independent MAS agents receive subspecialty roles, answer separately and vote.
The Decentralized MAS debates for at most 3 rounds from the same initial answers and votes over final answers~\cite{kim2026scaling,du2024debate}.

\subsection{Error correlation and majority accuracy}
Error correlation $\phi$ is the mean pairwise Pearson correlation of binary error indicators across items; pairs with constant indicators are omitted.
The effective number of independent agents is $N_{\mathrm{eff}}=9/(1+8\phi)$~\cite{kish1965survey}.
On yes/no items, a beta-binomial model predicts majority accuracy from mean agent accuracy $\bar p$ and intraclass correlation $\phi$:
\begin{equation}
  \alpha=\frac{\bar p(1-\phi)}{\phi},\qquad
  \beta=\frac{(1-\bar p)(1-\phi)}{\phi},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
  \begin{gathered}
    K\sim\mathrm{BetaBin}(9,\alpha,\beta),\\
    P(\text{majority correct})=P(K\geq 5).
  \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Multi-agent gain (MAS minus single-agent accuracy) splits into mean agent minus single-agent accuracy plus majority-vote minus mean agent accuracy.

\subsection{Probes}
Image probes use uniform gray blank images, reduced resolution, target or control occlusion, and paired images with opposite reference answers (\cref{tab:setup}).
Occlusion sensitivity is target minus control answer-change rate~\cite{zeiler2014visualizing}; pair accuracy requires both answers correct.
Shared wrong answers have at least 7 of 9 agents wrong.
Perception modules write query-agnostic reports without questions or options; reasoning modules answer from their own or a shared report without the image~\cite{qiao2024prism}.
Matched contrasts compare $\phi$ without versus with a shared component: role, contrast adjustment ($\gamma=0.7$ or CLAHE~\cite{zuiderveld1994clahe}), in-context examples (3 shared or private), report, perception module, reasoning module or model.
Conformity is the share of initially correct answers that become wrong after debate~\cite{asch1956conformity,demystifying_mad}.

\subsection{Data and models}
VQA-RAD and SLAKE use closed yes/no questions~\cite{lau2018vqarad,liu2021slake}; MedXpertQA-MM retains radiologic images~\cite{zuo2025medxpertqa}.
SLAKE-target has annotated target and control regions; SLAKE-pairs uses opposite-answer images~\cite{li2024naturalbench,tong2024eyes}.
Proprietary-model coverage appears in \cref{tab:setup}.
Open-weight models are MedGemma 4B, Qwen3-VL 8B, Qwen2-VL 7B, HuatuoGPT-Vision 7B and LLaVA-1.5 7B~\cite{sellergren2025medgemma,bai2025qwen3vl,wang2024qwen2vl,chen2024huatuovision,liu2024llava15}.
