\section{Conclusions}

For three proprietary VLMs, independent and discussion panels changed accuracy little relative to the single reader. Qwen 3.8 Flash gained more, mainly through more accurate panel readers. Error correlation limited nine-reader panels to an $N_{\mathrm{eff}}$ of 1.17 to 1.99. Language priors contributed to correlated errors on MedXpertQA-MM; the shared-report condition increased error correlation relative to the own-report condition. Conformity during discussion was greater for correct initial minority answers than for correct initial majority answers. A second AI reading lowered error correlation most when it came from a different model.
