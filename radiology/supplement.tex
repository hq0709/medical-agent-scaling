\section*{S2. Data collection}
Perception models generated findings reports before interpretation-model calls and were instructed to write at most 120 words. Median report lengths were 75 words for GPT-5 mini and Claude Haiku 4.5 and 90 for Gemini 3.7 Flash. Maximum lengths were 118, 121 and 127 words, respectively. Provider routing and token budgets remained fixed. Calls that failed with transient service errors were repeated until every item had a response. The automatic stop at more than 15\% unparsed answers was not applied to blank-image conditions.

\section*{S5. Text-only clinical questions}
In a companion analysis (unpublished data), 8 language models answered text-only clinical questions from MedQA, MedAgentsBench and the text set of MedXpertQA. The models answered as single readers and in panels with specialty roles. The 340 configurations comprised 96 Independent, 96 Centralized, 96 Decentralized and 52 Hybrid panels, with architecture names from Kim et al.\cite{kim2026scaling} Across the 288 Independent, Centralized and Decentralized configurations with 3 to 9 readers, mean initial-answer error correlation was 0.758. These architectures shared the initial answers and had identical initial-answer error correlations by design. With 9 readers, error correlation was 0.762, of the same magnitude as the imaging values. The effective number of independent readers ($N_{\mathrm{eff}}$) was 1.27. Dispersion across configurations was described by the standard deviation (SD). Across the 96 Decentralized configurations, error correlation was 0.758 (SD 0.117) before discussion and 0.990 (SD 0.010) after discussion. After discussion, $N_{\mathrm{eff}}$ was 1.01.
